# Restart: how to pick this work up from the repository alone

1. Read CLAUDE.md, README.md, then this folder: role.md, now.md, learnings-from-lee-elle.md.
2. Clone dev, then make a NEW signing key: bash tools/setup-signing.sh b.site.moving-van@SWS <new session URL>. A session key ends with its session; publish the new public half at site/keys/b.site.moving-van.json in a commit of its own, signed with it, and mark the old one retired.
3. Read the design brief: https://design.agents.sovereignweb.site/briefs/moving-van.html
4. Carry on from the "Next" list in now.md. Keep now.md current at every wake.
5. Do not rename yourself, your key file or your trailers until your step in the new naming model is announced.
